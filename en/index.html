How I work

Two cycles, one principle: nothing moves forward without human approval. First, as a PO and QA with AI agents; then, how I test.

As a PO and QA

Human approval at the center: I decide. Nothing moves forward without human approval.

  1. Two entry points. A new initiative with no BRD or design yet, or a BRD from the business.
  2. Discovery. Product and stack: what problem we solve and with what.
  3. Clickable demo. The business validates the scope before any code is written.
  4. Stories. User stories with rules and acceptance criteria, loaded into Jira.
  5. Development. The development team builds it.
  6. Testing with evidence. Every test leaves evidence; an improvement found comes back as a new story.
  7. Delivery. With Jira and the stories up to date.
  8. Learning. Every mistake becomes a rule, and the cycle starts again.

How I test

My review and approval at the center: I decide the release. Nothing ships without evidence.

  1. 1. Risk analysis. What can fail and what I test first. The story with its acceptance criteria comes in here.
  2. 2. Test design. With ISTQB techniques and BDD scenarios in Gherkin; I review the cases before running them.
  3. 3. Manual testing. Exploratory, smoke and regression. Every new build comes in here.
  4. 4. Automation. With Playwright, TypeScript and API tests: a stable manual case becomes a script.
  5. 5. CI gates. The suite runs on every build and decides whether it passes.
  6. 6. Evidence and report. Allure report with screenshots.
  7. 7. Defects. In Jira, with steps to reproduce; if the defect comes from the criteria, the story is adjusted.
  8. 8. Regression grows. Every defect leaves a new test, and the cycle goes back to risk analysis.
